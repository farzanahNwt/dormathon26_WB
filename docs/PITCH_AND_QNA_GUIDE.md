## 🎯 Part 4: Judge Q&A Battlecard (Tough Questions & Winning Responses)

### Q1: *"How is this using Tencent WorkBuddy? Isn't it just calling APIs directly?"*
> **Answer:**  
> *"No, that is the difference between a linear script and a WorkBuddy agent.  
> In LAICIs, the incoming input and user goal are unstructured. The user doesn't specify 'run PyTorch' or 'check PDRM'.  
> Tencent WorkBuddy ingests the input, reads our registered MCP tool definitions at `/api/workbuddy/mcp`, and autonomously formulates an execution plan. If the input is an SMS with a bank account, WorkBuddy dispatches the PDRM mule checker and Gemini; if it's an image, it orchestrates our PyTorch CNN, ELA analyzer, and vision reasoning in parallel.  
> WorkBuddy is the cognitive dispatcher and orchestration engine of the entire system."*

---

### Q2: *"Why did you use Google Gemini alongside Tencent WorkBuddy?"*
> **Answer:**  
> *"We designed LAICIs following best-practice agentic architecture: **Orchestrator vs. Specialized Tool**.  
> Tencent WorkBuddy acts as the sovereign agent orchestrator managing tool selection, system state, and security protocols via Tencent Cloud credentials.  
> Within that orchestrated toolchain, WorkBuddy calls Google Gemini (`gemini-3-flash-preview`) as a specialized multimodal and linguistic reasoner. Gemini analyzes deceptive semantics, social engineering, and subtle visual artifacts, while local PyTorch CNN weights handle low-level pixel feature maps. This division of labor delivers maximum speed and precision."*

---

### Q3: *"How do you prevent hallucinations in your financial exposure numbers?"*
> **Answer:**  
> *"We strictly prohibit LLMs from freely inventing financial exposure numbers.  
> Financial risk is calculated by our deterministic `FinancialRiskAssessor` engine, calibrated to official 2026 PDRM Commercial Crime Investigation Department (CCID) actuarial benchmarks.  
> If an input is a pure media file without financial solicitations, the engine enforces **RM 0.00** exposure. If a payment demand or bank account is detected, the engine parses the exact transaction amount and calculates exposure based on verified fraud risk tiers. This guarantees 100% mathematical reliability."*

---

### Q4: *"What happens if an external API (like Sightengine or Gemini) fails or times out during an attack?"*
> **Answer:**  
> *"LAICIs is architected with dual-layer resilient failover.  
> First, our primary deepfake CNN model (`best_model.pth`, EfficientNetV2-S) and our Error Level Analysis (ELA) engine run 100% locally on Python microservices with zero external network dependencies.  
> Second, our agentic runner wraps all external API calls with non-blocking try/except handlers and timeout controllers. If Gemini or Sightengine is unavailable, the agent flags the limitation in the audit log, re-weights the composite score across available local models, and still delivers a validated forensic report without crashing."*

---

### Q5: *"Can your report be used as evidence in a Malaysian court or police report?"*
> **Answer:**  
> *"Yes. Under Section 90A of the Malaysian Evidence Act 1950, computer-generated documents are admissible if accompanied by a certificate of integrity.  
> Every LAICIs investigation generates:  
> 1. A cryptographic SHA-256 hash of the submitted evidence and model outputs stored in an immutable audit ledger.  
> 2. An official PDRM CCID Police Dossier specifically formatted with suspect bank accounts, mule registry flags, and forensic timestamps ready for submission to the National Scam Response Centre (NSRC 997) or Commercial Crime Department."*

---

### Q6: *"How does LAICIs protect user privacy under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)?"*
> **Answer:**  
> *"LAICIs enforces privacy by design:  
> 1. Local execution: Media files can be inspected completely on-premises using our local PyTorch and ELA models without sending biometric data to external clouds.  
> 2. PII Redaction: Bank accounts and phone numbers are hashed when queried against the local Semak Mule database.
