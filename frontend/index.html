04 / QUESTIONSCLEAR ANSWERS

Trust needs evidence.

Can LAICIs prove that an image is fake? +

No. Machine-learning scores are indicators, not proof. Results should state the model, limitations, and next verification steps.

What happens to my uploads? +

This prototype inspects basic file information in the browser. If a backend is configured, media is sent to the service you provide. Review its privacy policy before uploading personal content.

Does the phone version have a dashboard? +

No. On narrow screens, LAICIs prioritizes the detection tool, with secondary marketing sections hidden for a focused mobile experience.
